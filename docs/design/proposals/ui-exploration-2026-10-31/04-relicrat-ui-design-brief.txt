RELICRAT UI EXPLORATION — DESIGN BRIEF
Prepared October 31, 2026 | Status: EXPLORATORY CONCEPTS

Purpose
Carry forward the three original mockup exports from “UI exploration.” These
are discussion references, not approved production designs or brand masters.
The PNGs are preserved without visual edits and renamed to make status clear.

Direction
Create modern, polished, collection-first software for comics, trading cards,
records, art, toys, books, and other collectibles. Prioritize imagery, readable
metadata, efficient search/filtering, condition, and provenance. Use forest-green
navigation, clean ivory/neutral surfaces, and restrained burgundy accents.
Avoid sepia dashboards, blanket paper textures, and ornate antique-shop styling.
The intended next-iteration format is three separate full 4:3 desktop screens.
The supplied originals are crops from one exploration, not finished 4:3 layouts:
home 724 x 619 px; collection 724 x 619 px; details 1448 x 467 px. Do not stretch
them to imply that composition work is complete.

Screens
01-relicrat-home-EXPLORATORY.png
  Home/dashboard: collection overview, recent additions, category navigation,
  search, and an add-item action. Let the collection lead; use mascot moments
  selectively rather than allowing illustration to overwhelm inventory.
02-relicrat-collection-EXPLORATORY.png
  Collection browser: image-first grid with category tabs, filters, sorting,
  and alternate views. Fictional collectibles provide illustrative content.
03-relicrat-item-details-EXPLORATORY.png
  Item details/provenance: large collectible image, metadata, condition/grade,
  owned copies, acquisition history, provenance events, and supporting images.
  Counts, prices, grades, certificates, and history are illustrative placeholders;
  they do not establish verified authenticity, valuation, or implemented features.

Canonical brand references — retain in place, do not replace or duplicate
The existing Project source RELICRAT_BRAND(1).md is the canonical creative-
direction document (v1.0; production specifications remain in progress).
It identifies these required visual references:

  a_clean_character_design_and_iconography_presentat.png

    Approved modern-collectibles creative direction; individual assets exploratory.
These images are referenced here, not re-exported or duplicated in this package.
Their presence as standalone Project sources was not verified.

Preserve the established mascot: charcoal-gray fur, cream muzzle, oversized
rounded pink ears, swept-back fur, pink nose/tail, burgundy waistcoat, cream
rolled-sleeve shirt, brown trousers, and leather satchel. The primary mascot
reference and written brand constraints take precedence over inconsistencies
in these mockups. Do not extract a new mascot, icon, logo, font, or palette
from generated UI pixels. Final wordmark, typography, exact colors, and vector
masters remain unapproved. Generated collectible artwork is not production art.
